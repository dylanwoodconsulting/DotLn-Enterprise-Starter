# Add shooting and bullets to the soldier scene

The soldier scene's pieces could move, form up and route round walls, but nothing on the field could be shot, ended or marked by anything else moving. This change adds a Fight group to the rail. Select pieces, choose Aiming and shooting and click another glyph, and streaks in each shooter's role colour cross the field, a spark marks each hit, the instruments count shots, hits and accuracy, and the target's text row counts the hits it has taken. A streak stops at a wall or an obstacle and leaves a mark. Hits to knock out retires a piece on the hit that reaches the count, and its squad lays its pattern out again round the members still standing.

## What changed

- **The engine.** `projectiles.ts` makes a streak an entity of the simulation state, with its owner, position, velocity, range left, radius and drag. Streaks advance before the pieces move each frame, end on the first standing piece other than their owner, on a wall or an obstacle (the same swept test the geometry uses) or when their range is spent, and count each hit on the shooter and on the piece struck. `aiming.ts` holds the two arcade aims over the board's own recorded animation: The firing solution and Moving targets read one recorded later pose of the marked piece and time the streak's clip to it, drag stretches that clip, and Iterative targeting reads a later pose up to its Later cues setting. One clip of the board is recorded per frame and shared by every shooter, and the clock identifies each cue on replay. A unit gains `hits`, a `down` activity and its launcher settings; a downed unit is not steered, collides with nothing, draws no rays and is skipped by later steps. `formation.ts` keeps the pattern, spacing and slot costs a squad was formed with, so a two-level squad that loses a member to a knockout is laid out again for those standing and reassigned under its costs, and a fixed squad whose leader goes down is led by the member that held the next slot; a member taken out by a later tool still leaves its slot empty. The frame hash folds streaks in after the units, so a frame without streaks hashes as before. The metrics gain shots, hits, accuracy and down units.
- **The catalog rows.** Five AI for Games rows gain typed transforms: Aiming and shooting (`combat.fire`), Projectile trajectory (`combat.projectile`), and The firing solution, Iterative targeting and Moving targets (`combat.aim`). The closed transform enum, `TransformKind` and the three lists of scene kinds grow by the three kinds, and the catalog summary is rewritten to what the check computes, with its date unchanged. A fire step carries its target as a number, the mark's place in the scene's list or 0 for the objective point, and a stored step must give rate and shot speed above zero.
- **The page.** The rail's Fight group holds the five tools. The inspector's fire view chooses the target from a list or by a click on a glyph, and its preview reads the pose frame, the planned clip and whether range cuts it short. The canvas draws streaks in the owner's role colour, sparks on hits, chipped marks on geometry and retired pieces dim; the text layer counts streaks in flight, sparks and marks, and each row its hits. A trial compares accuracy as a series and draws the earlier branch's streaks dotted. Every new string is a message, in the scene and on the five rows' catalog pages.
- **A route display repair.** After Restart, a path tool's inspector and Show search follow the selected units' recorded routes through playback and pause, and preview Apply only before a route exists or after a path parameter is edited; a parameter commit on an unapplied tool adds no step.

## Regenerated expected data

- `trial-known-outcome.json` and `walls-known-outcome.json` were regenerated because every known outcome now carries four more metric series. Compared with the files at the base, every frame-hash array is unchanged and the only difference in each is the shots, hits, accuracy and down-units series, zeros and nulls, since a run with no shooting moves exactly as it did.
- `range-known-outcome.json` is new: three shooters firing at a standing piece across clear ground, and the same volley with a wall drawn between them. In the first branch the target is hit before the last frame; in the second every streak ends on the wall and the target takes no hit. The fixture check lists it.
- Six scene visual baselines (`scene-360`, `scene-768`, `scene-1440` and the three `scene-flocking` images) were regenerated on macOS for the wider instrument strip and the Fight group, and three are new: `scene-volley-360`, `scene-volley-1440` and `scene-knockout-row-360`. No other baseline changed.

## Review

The final review read the whole change and made no source change. Three rough edges, all in the knockout step, are recorded for a later change: Aiming and shooting reopens on its defaults after another tool, so its Fire at must be chosen again; a knockout set late in a session counts hits already taken; and Down units counts only the selected pieces that are down. The hit marker's look, a spark alone or a spark with a progress ring, awaits the operator's pick, and the spark ships. The draft of this pull request had no review, comment or thread, and its checks, visual and title runs passed.
