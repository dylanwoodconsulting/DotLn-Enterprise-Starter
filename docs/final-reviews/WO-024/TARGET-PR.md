# Add Gödel, Escher, Bach and Team Topologies studies, each a relationship map to step through

The catalog listed 573 concepts from Gödel, Escher, Bach and 637 from Team Topologies with none of them studied, and the `relations` drawing family was a name with nothing behind it. This change gives each work ten studies and builds that family. A reader chooses either work on the shelf, opens a study and steps through a small map of named things and the directed relationships between them: a stack of levels read from the bottom up, with an arrow returning outside the stack where the idea is a loop, or a graph in two or three columns. The sentence under the controls names the box or arrow in focus and says what happens there. Each study gives the idea in plain words, a worked example, ordinary pseudocode, public treatments and references, its limits, nearby concepts as links and, after the map, where a comparison with DotLn or this app holds and where it stops.

## What changed

- **The relations family.** `relationsModel` in `libs/features/tools/src/lib/studies/relations.ts` takes one example as data (nodes, directed relationships, ordered steps, named moments, a caption and an optional opening sample) and builds the whole drawing from it: the layout, the box or arrow in focus at each step, the reading, the moments and the legend. `relations-layout.ts` places the nodes as levels or in two or three columns and ends each arrow at the edge of its box, with long vertical links on an outer rail and reciprocal links on opposite sides. The builder refuses a relationship, step or moment that names something the example lacks, a repeated id and a step that names both a node and a relationship, so a mistake fails when the model builds. The family's composite draws the map as an SVG whose accessible name is the selected step's reading. The study composite gains the family's case: an opening sample with a prediction the reader can reveal, the worked example shown open, the instructions for reading the arrows above the map and the comparison after it.
- **The controls.** The shared scrubber can mark the named moment that matches the current step (`aria-current="step"`) and, at a drawing's last step, offer Back to the start, which returns to step 0 and keeps keyboard focus on the slider. Both are inputs that only the relations family sets, so the other families' controls are unchanged.
- **Twenty studies.** Gödel, Escher, Bach: Levels of description, Chunking, Consistency, Gödel numbering, Decision procedure, Isomorphism, Sentence G, Jumping out of the system, Active symbols and Strange loop. Team Topologies: X-as-a-Service mode, Collaboration mode, Facilitating mode, Conway's law (1968), Reverse Conway manoeuvre, Homomorphic force, Team-scoped flow, Platform as a product, Organizational sensing and Seven-to-nine maximum effective team size. Each record is `concept-study-v1` with every authored field a message key in the new lazy catalogs `studyGeb/en.json` and `studyTt/en.json`. Sixteen studies are one data module each, passed to the builder, with a spec that loads the record. Four run a mechanism: Chunking groups a five-event recording at a width the reader sets, Gödel numbering encodes and decodes words over a two-letter alphabet and tests a candidate number, Decision procedure decides reachability in a small lantern relay with one wire the reader can connect, and Conway's law copies a team graph's design contacts into component dependencies with three contacts to switch. Each of the four has a language-free simulation, a model entry that passes the run's results to the builder, and a spec that loads the record and asserts the example's result. No study has a presentation module of its own.
- **Wiring.** The work chunks `geb.ts` and `tt.ts` with their loader entries, twenty model registrations, and one table from work to message catalog (`study-scopes.json`) that the app and the tooling both read.
- **Checks.** The study check holds a relations drawing of a model that runs to that same module, as it does a states drawing, and requires the illustrative mark on an RxJS reading in the two new works. The message check refuses a record or a registered model that reads a key from another work's catalog, which a fresh route would not have loaded, and screens the opening sample's fields as visible text. The corpus test pins the two works' twenty ids, their family and which four carry an executable module.
- **Documents.** `docs/dev-reference/architecture/catalog.md` describes the builder, the family and the two coverage rows, and `docs/ai-reference/catalog-rules.md` gains the rule for a relations study.

## Review

The final review read the whole change and corrected one comment in the study check. Nothing else changed after verification. Two rough edges are recorded for a later change. In the X-as-a-Service map the Preview API's reply and Checkout's report meet and cross at Checkout's right edge beside a third arrowhead: the map stays readable and the sentence under the controls names each arrow, but that edge is crowded. The browser check of Organizational sensing reads the trial's duration in the step that approves the trial and not in the step that reports its results.

The drawing's look was settled through thirteen rounds of rendered pairs. The stack with a returning arrow, emphasis on the selected arrow alone, the reading below the controls, an arrow without a printed verb, the return to the start, the marked current moment and several studies' openings were each chosen from a pair. Two rounds ended with neither candidate chosen, and the comparisons were closed after the last of them. This pull request had no draft, so its checks run for the first time on this head.
